## B) Strongest pain points

1. **Thin liquidity and bad execution in pools.**
   - Evidence: xStocks trades 86% through passive pools; Robinhood Chain small orders cost 428.9bps; SLVR says "almost nobody" quotes the pools; SAFIX and Brian say the same.
   - Who: traders, lenders, apps.
   - Solved? Partly: Backpack with Sunrise's market makers (Solana), Standard, SLVR and Retain (Robinhood). Other xStocks pools on Solana are still thin.
2. **Wash or pass-through volume hides real demand.**
   - Evidence: 70% of Solana equity volume is in-and-out within a day. Robinhood Chain turned over 26× its $167M of tokens in one week, and about half its 30-day volume is stock-vs-meme pairs (Allium).
   - Who: investors, integrators, agents.
   - Solved? Only research reports and one GitHub repo.
3. **Idle holdings.**
   - Evidence: ~9% of Solana RWAs are used in DeFi; stock collateral is ≈$36–60M; $243M has been held for more than a week.
   - Who: holders.
   - Solved? Partly: Kamino, Jupiter Lend and Loopscale on Solana; Morpho, SAFIX and Spine elsewhere. The blockers are liquidation depth and off-hours pricing.
4. **No reliable price when US markets are closed, and no halt handling.**
   - Evidence: 63% of volume is off-hours and 17% on weekends; FloatSheets; the SEC order requires halt syncing.
   - Who: LPs, lenders, perp venues.
   - Solved? Partly: Pyth's stock and xStock feeds, Chainlink, Backpack futures, Syra and afterhoursoracle.xyz.
5. **Corporate actions and market-state data.**
   - Evidence: FloatSheets' list of events; the xStocks API has multipliers and corporate actions; Token-2022 quirks.
   - Who: every integrator.
   - Solved? Each issuer publishes its own; there's no cross-issuer standard.
6. **Fragmented legal claims and pricing.**
   - Evidence: xStocks are debt securities; Backpack gives a brokerage entitlement redeemable for the real share; Robinhood's are debt securities; Coinbase's are real shares; PreStocks and Tessera are exposure through an SPV. Pre-IPO implied valuations differ 1.7–2.3× between issuers.
   - Who: retail, agents, lenders.
   - Solved? Prism and Syra, early.
7. **Emerging-market onboarding.**
   - Evidence: Ramzy; Base's request for builders; the Dangote rail works but only ≈$15K came through it net.
   - Who: Nigerian retail, freelancers paid in USD.
   - Solved? NectarFi/GetEquity for local IPOs, and Luno (custodial). No self-custodial path from naira or USD earnings into global stocks.
8. **No consumer layer.**
   - Evidence: Tenev; the Base roundtable consensus.
   - Solved? Very crowded: Daybreak (40 users), Slyz, tipping and gifting apps.
9. **Stock-paired token launches.**
   - Evidence: StonkFun $1.2B cumulative, Pump.fun Custom Pairs, OTC; Meteora's bounty asks for something that "outlasts the meme-stock meta".
   - Solved? Saturated.
10. **Hedging and yield for holders.**
    - Evidence: Derive, Algod, Horinek, Note Systems, DividendX.
    - Solved? Not on Solana. The moat is market makers and liquidity.
11. **Perps demand far exceeds spot, and Solana is absent.**
    - Evidence: RWA perps $117.3B/month; Solana ≈1% of perps.
    - Solved? Hyperliquid/TradeXYZ and Lighter. Galaxy suggests using stocks as perp margin on Solana, which is capital-heavy.
12. **Agent tooling** (structured data, guardrails, pay-per-call).
    - Solved? Xona, Syra, stonkfly, Jupiter skills. Crowded, and there's no evidence yet that anyone pays.
13. **US compliance plumbing** (permissioning, trading-volume caps, halts, 10-minute trade reporting).
    - Solved? No middleware vendor is named. The venue itself must be a US person.
14. **Proving the token equals the share.**
    - Evidence: Condia and Afriflux on the Dangote token's mint/freeze/multisig powers; the xStocks proof-of-reserves API.
    - Solved? Only Afriflux, for Nigeria.

## D) What a first pass would likely miss

- **Robinhood Chain's lead rests on weak flow:** half is meme pairs, gas is subsidized until Sep 29, and it turns over 26× its supply weekly. Solana's execution is measurably better. Re-check volumes after Sep 29.
- **The Dangote "Solana dominant rail" story is ≈$15K net.** Buyers hold receipts the issuer can mint and freeze, and there is no secondary market. Onchain demand for Nigerian local IPOs is unproven.
- **SEC order nuances:**
  - It does not limit what non-security crypto asset a stock can be paired with, so meme pairs aren't excluded.
  - The Tier 2 cap is 2.5% of daily volume and 250 symbols; Brian cited only the 0.25% Tier 1 figure.
  - Quote requests and offchain functions are allowed.
  - No new issuance and no leverage on the venue.
  - It expires Sep 17, 2031.
- **BlackRock's paper doesn't endorse tokenized stocks or Solana.**
- **Supply estimates conflict:** $2.5B (Frictionless), $2.8B (kenn_ronin), $3B (Brian), $3.3B (Allium's report title).
- **The fair-price wedge is crowded:** Syra's fair-value desk, EarnFi and afterhoursoracle.xyz all overlap with your Stocklana fair-price app.
- **Deadlines:** Stocklana closes today 4pm ET; Colosseum World's Fair Oct 12; Project Harmonia Oct 24.
